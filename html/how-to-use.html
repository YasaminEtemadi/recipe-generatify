How to Use

1

Login, or create an account if you haven't already

2

Click the "Find a new recipe" button

3

Select the ingredients that are currently available to you, or ingredients that you want to use, from the dropdown menus

4

Select the type of meal that you want your recipe to be made for (ex. breakfast)

5

Press the "Generate recipe" button and watch as the perfect recipe is generated for you
