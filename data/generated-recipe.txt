Here is a recipe for **Classic Cream Puffs (Profiteroles) with Chocolate Glaze**. This recipe uses **only** ingredients from your allowed list (no salt, oil, or water added).

---

### **Classic Cream Puffs with Chocolate Glaze**

Yields: ~12–15 pastry puffs  
Prep time: 25 minutes  
Bake time: 35 minutes  

---

### **Ingredients**

#### **For the Pastry Shells (Choux Pastry):**
* **Milk:** 1 cup
* **Butter:** 1/2 cup (1 stick)
* **Granulated sugar:** 1 tablespoon
* **All-purpose flour:** 1 cup
* **Eggs:** 4 large eggs

#### **For the Vanilla Cream Filling:**
* **Cream:** 1 cup (heavy cream)
* **Granulated sugar:** 3 tablespoons
* **Vanilla extract:** 1 teaspoon

#### **For the Chocolate Fudge Glaze:**
* **Condensed milk:** 1/2 cup
* **Cocoa powder:** 3 tablespoons
* **Butter:** 2 tablespoons
* **Milk:** 1 to 2 tablespoons (if needed to thin)

---

### **Instructions**

#### **1. Make the Pastry Shells:**
1. **Preheat your oven** to 400°F (200°C). Grease a baking sheet lightly with a tiny bit of butter or line it with baking paper.
2. **Heat liquid & butter:** In a saucepan over medium heat, combine the **1 cup milk**, **1/2 cup butter**, and **1 tablespoon granulated sugar**. Bring to a gentle boil.
3. **Add flour:** Once boiling, remove the saucepan from heat. Add all **1 cup of all-purpose flour** at once. Stir vigorously with a wooden spoon until a smooth dough ball forms and pulls away from the sides of the pan.
4. **Dry the dough:** Return the saucepan to low heat for 1–2 minutes, stirring continuously, to cook off excess moisture. Transfer dough to a mixing bowl and let it cool for 5 minutes.
5. **Add eggs:** Add the **eggs** one at a time, beating thoroughly after each addition until completely smooth and glossy before adding the next. 
6. **Shape & Bake:** Drop dollops of dough (about 2 tablespoons each) onto your prepared baking sheet, leaving space between them. 
7. **Bake:** Bake at 400°F (200°C) for 20 minutes. **Without opening the oven door**, reduce heat to 350°F (175°C) and bake for another 10–15 minutes until golden brown and firm to the touch. 
8. Turn off the oven, crack the door slightly, and let them sit inside for 5 minutes. Remove and cool completely on a wire rack.

#### **2. Make the Vanilla Cream Filling:**
1. In a cold mixing bowl, combine the cold **heavy cream**, **3 tablespoons granulated sugar**, and **1 teaspoon vanilla extract**.
2. Beat vigorously with a whisk or electric mixer until stiff peaks form. Refrigerate until ready to use.

#### **3. Make the Chocolate Glaze:**
1. In a small saucepan over low heat, whisk together the **condensed milk**, **cocoa powder**, **2 tablespoons butter**, and **1 tablespoon milk**. 
2. Stir continuously until the butter melts and the mixture turns smooth, dark, and glossy (about 2–3 minutes). If it’s too thick, stir in an extra tablespoon of milk. Remove from heat.

#### **4. Assemble:**
1. Slice the cooled pastry shells horizontally in half (or poke a hole in the bottom of each shell).
2. Spoon or pipe the **Vanilla Cream Filling** generously into the bottom half of each shell, then place the top half back on.
3. Drizzle or dip the tops with the warm **Chocolate Fudge Glaze**. Serve immediately!
